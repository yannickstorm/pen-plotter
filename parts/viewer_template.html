Fitting order

  1. Screw the arm to the motor with two M3 screws.
  2. On the carrier, fit the lower (Y) switch first. Its screws go in through the two holes in the upper shelf.
  3. Fit the upper (X) switch on top.
  4. Lay the carrier on the arm's clamp pad. Put an M3 screw through each pair of crossing slots, with a nut under the pad. Slide, test the switches, tighten.

Print the arm with its base down and the carrier upside down, upper plate on the bed. The arm is long and thin, so use four or more walls. The click side is the switch's long side, facing the axis direction. Switch thickness (5.8 mm) is assumed. The Y switch has 6 mm free behind it for its solder pins.
