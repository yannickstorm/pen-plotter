Reading the printed parts

Each scale is engraved, 1 mm per tick, 0 is the nominal position, plus is towards +X and +Y. Type your two readings into the boxes above to get the final positions.

  1. Y: on the arm's block, the +Y end edge of the cross-slide sits next to a numbered tick.
  2. X: on top of the cross-slide, the −X end edge of the carrier's plate sits next to a tick. The longest tick is 0, the medium ones are ±4 and ±8.

The button Read X · Y zooms to these scales. There is no Z slide: the heights come from the design.

Fitting order

  1. Screw the arm to the motor with two M3 screws.
  2. Press an M3 heat-set insert into each of the two locking holes: on the +X face of the arm's block and on the +Y side of the cross-slide. Set screws: about M3 × 10.
  3. Fit each switch from the front or the back into its holder, on the shelf under the plate. Its two M2 screws go in through the holes in the plate above it.
  4. Slide the cross-slide into the arm's block along Y. Slide the carrier into the cross-slide along X.
  5. Move the carrier to the reading you want and lock each slide with its set screw. The slides are a loose fit, so they hold only when locked.

Prototype settings: no supports needed. Print the arm flat (base on the bed), the cross-slide and the carrier upside down (top faces on the bed). Two walls and 10 to 15 % infill are enough. If the slides bind or wobble, change DT_CL (0.3 mm clearance per side) and reprint. The click side is the switch's long side, facing the lever direction. The lever is assumed to stick out 2.5 mm in front of the switch, and the switch thickness (5.8 mm) is assumed. The X shelf reaches 0.9 mm below the motor face level.
