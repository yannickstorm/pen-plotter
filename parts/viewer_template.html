Fitting order

  1. Screw the base to the motor with two M3 screws.
  2. Press an M3 nut into each hex pocket on top of the Y arm's thin tail.
  3. Lay the Y arm on the tongue and screw it on from below with two M3 × 5 screws through the tongue.
  4. Fit each switch with two self-tapping M2 screws, M2 × 8 on the base and M2 × 10 on the arm.

Both parts print flat, underside on the bed, with no supports. Two walls and 10 to 15 % infill are enough. The click side is the switch's long side, facing the lever direction. The lever is assumed to stick out 2.5 mm in front of the switch, and the switch thickness (5.8 mm) is assumed. The base is 2.1 mm thick, exactly the X switch's resting height, so the X switch sits on the plate.
