Fitting order

  1. Screw the base to the motor with two M3 screws.
  2. Fit the lower (Y) switch first. Its screws go in through the two holes in the upper shelf.
  3. Fit the upper (X) switch on top.

Print with the base down. The arm is long and thin, so use four or more walls. The click side is the switch's long side, facing the axis direction. Switch size and pilot holes are assumed, not measured.
